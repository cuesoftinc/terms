the height you give. They depend on the photos, the lighting, your
clothing and your pose, and they are not a tailor’s tape measure. Check
them before you rely on them for an order, and correct any that are wrong:
the measurements attached to an order are the ones you approved.

## How an Order Works

1. **Request and quote.** A customer requests an outfit; the designer
   quotes a price. A quote not acted on within **7 days** expires.
2. **Payment.** The customer pays the full quote on **Paystack’s**
   checkout. Cuesoft holds the payment until the order ends; the designer
   is not paid at this point.
3. **Work and delivery.** If the designer has not started within **14
   days** of payment, the customer is refunded automatically. Once the
   designer marks the order shipped, the customer confirms delivery, or it
   is confirmed automatically **14 days** later.
4. **Payout.** On delivery, the designer is paid the quote less
   Apparule’s **10% fee**.
5. **Disputes.** Either party can open a dispute any time before delivery
   is confirmed. A dispute freezes the payout until our support team
   resolves it, either releasing the payout to the designer or refunding
   the customer.
6. **Refunds** are of the full amount, started within **1 business day**
   of the decision, and Cuesoft bears the payment provider’s refund fee.
   How long the money takes to reach you depends on your bank.
7. **Keep payments on Apparule.** Do not share payment links or ask to be
   paid outside an order: payments made outside Apparule are not covered
   by its holding, refund or dispute process.
8. We hold a payment so that the order can be completed fairly. Holding it
   does not make Cuesoft the seller, a party to the contract for the outfit, or
   a guarantor of it.
9. Be honest in a dispute and give the facts and pictures we ask for. People
   in our support team resolve a dispute, and their decision settles who is
   paid or refunded inside Apparule. It does not take away any right you have
   under the law, or against your bank or card issuer.
10. Paystack processes the payment, the refund and the payout under its own
    terms and privacy policy. Card details never enter Apparule, and Cuesoft
    never receives them.
