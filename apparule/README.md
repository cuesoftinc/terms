1. **Request and quote.** A customer requests an outfit; the designer
   quotes a price. A quote not acted on within **7 days** expires.
2. **Payment.** The customer pays the full quote on **Paystack’s**
   checkout. Cuesoft holds the payment until the order ends; the designer
   is not paid at this point.
3. **Work and delivery.** If the designer has not started within **14
   days** of payment, the customer is refunded automatically. Once the
   designer marks the order shipped, the customer confirms delivery, or it
   is confirmed automatically **14 days** later.
4. **Payout.** On delivery, the designer is paid the quote less
   Apparule’s **10% fee**.
5. **Disputes.** Either party can open a dispute any time before delivery
   is confirmed. A dispute freezes the payout until our support team
   resolves it, either releasing the payout to the designer or refunding
   the customer.
6. **Refunds** are of the full amount, started within **1 business day**
   of the decision, and Cuesoft bears the payment provider’s refund fee.

7. **Keep payments on Apparule.** Do not share payment links or ask to be
