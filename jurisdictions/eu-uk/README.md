This page states how these Terms operate if you are in the EU, the EEA or
the UK. Your counterparty is Cuesoft Nigeria Limited (see
[Nigeria](../nigeria/)) unless a signed agreement says otherwise.

## Consumer protections are preserved

If you use the Websites or join a programme as a **consumer** in the
EU/EEA or UK:

1. Nothing in these Terms deprives you of protections granted by
   provisions of your local law that cannot be derogated from by
   agreement — including unfair-contract-terms protections and statutory
   guarantees.
2. Nothing in the [Nigeria dispute clause](../nigeria/) prevents you from
   bringing or defending proceedings in the courts of your own country of
   residence, where your local law gives you that right.
3. The [limitation of liability](../../legal/liability/) applies to you
   only to the extent your local law permits.

## Distance-contract information

- The trader for EU/UK purposes is identified, with addresses and contact

- **No payment is taken on the Websites**; paid programmes (CueTA™) are
  contracted off-site under a written offer that states the price,
  the service and its duration before you commit.
- Where EU/UK distance-selling law grants you a 14-day withdrawal right

  early) are stated in the offer of admission; the
  [refund terms](../../programmes/cueta/) operate alongside it, and the
  more protective provision prevails.
