# CueLABS™: Open-source Internship Terms

These terms govern applications to and participation in CueLABS™, the
Cuesoft open-source internship programme. The signed internship agreement
governs each internship and prevails over this page wherever they differ.

## Scope and precedence

1. These terms cover your application to CueLABS™, your participation in it
   and your use of cuelabs.cuesoft.io and the applicant portal for those
   purposes.
2. They sit alongside the general [Terms of Service](../../), which also apply
   to the CueLABS™ website and to your Cuesoft account. For anything concerning
   CueLABS™, the signed internship agreement prevails over these terms, and
   these terms prevail over the Terms of Service.
3. **Cuesoft**, **we**, **us** and **our** mean Cuesoft Inc. and Cuesoft
   Nigeria Limited, as the [Terms of Service](../../) define them. Which of
   them you deal with, and the law and forum that apply, are set out in the
   [Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/),
   [United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/) and
   [EU/UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/) pages.
4. In these terms:
   - **Cuesoft account** is the account you sign up for, sign in to and
     manage at account.cuesoft.io.
   - **Signed agreement** is a separate written agreement between you and
     Cuesoft, here the internship agreement.
   - **Your content** is anything you submit, upload, post, type or record
     on the CueLABS™ website, the applicant portal or the programme’s
     channels.
   - **AI output** is anything an AI feature (including Ace) generates.

## Applying

1. Applications are made on the CueLABS™ website and create no contract;
   an internship exists only when both parties sign the internship
   agreement.
2. Selection is at Cuesoft’s discretion, on the application, the
   candidate’s public work and any assessment conversation. Decisions are
   made by humans.
3. You must be at least 18 years old to apply.

## Your application and the applicant portal

1. What you tell us in your application, in your CV and in any assessment
   conversation must be true and complete. You must have the right to share
   your CV and any work you point us to.
2. We record when you accept these terms. Submitting an application does not
   reserve a place.
3. After you apply, you follow your application and, once you are a member,
   your work in the applicant portal. You sign in with your Cuesoft account
   at account.cuesoft.io: keep your sign-in details secret, do not share
   them, and tell us at [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) if you
   think someone else is using them.
4. On the contract page you confirm that you have signed. The signing link is
   a Google Docs or DocuSign link that Cuesoft emails you, and that service’s
   own terms apply to what you do there.
5. Members can see a leaderboard that lists members by name with their
   commit and merged pull request counts. By becoming a member you accept
   that.
6. Providing false information or forged documents can end your application
   or your internship at once.

## The engagement

1. **Unpaid, by design.** CueLABS™ internships are unpaid engagements
   governed by a signed internship agreement. They create **no employment
   relationship**: no wages, benefits, leave entitlements or employment
   rights arise, in any jurisdiction.
2. **Data allowance.** Cuesoft covers interns’ connectivity costs through
   a monthly data allowance stated in the internship agreement.
3. **Term and notice.** Internships run for the fixed term stated in the
   agreement, three or six months, and either party may end one on the
   notice the agreement states.
4. **What interns get.** Supervised production experience on live,
   MIT-licensed products with public commit histories under the intern’s
   own GitHub identity; sprint-based mentorship against the published
   [engineering standard](https://github.com/cuesoftinc/oss-engineering-standards);
   and a written performance record at mid-term and end of term.
5. **Equipment.** Interns work remotely on their own equipment, secured to
   the standards of the
   [handbook policies](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/).

## Intern status

1. You are an intern, not an employee, agent or representative of Cuesoft.
   You may say accurately that you are a CueLABS™ intern, and you may not
   speak for Cuesoft, sign for it or bind it.
2. The data allowance is a contribution to your connectivity costs. It is not
   a wage or payment for services. Bank details for it are entered on your
   Cuesoft account at account.cuesoft.io, not on the CueLABS™ website, and you
   are responsible for keeping them correct.
3. You manage your own time, your studies and your other commitments. Before
   you sign, make sure nothing in your studies, your employment or any other
   agreement stops you from doing the work or from assigning its work product
   to Cuesoft.

## Equipment and security

1. You provide your own equipment, and you keep it to the standards of the
   handbook’s [BYOD policy](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/byod/) and
   [cybersecurity policy](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/cyber/):
   an up-to-date operating system with security patches, full-disk
   encryption and a lock screen.
2. Use strong, unique passwords in a password manager and two-factor
   authentication on every Cuesoft account. Never share a password. Keep
   secrets such as keys and tokens out of code, repositories and chat.
3. Keep confidential files and client data only in approved company systems,
   never in personal cloud storage.
4. Report phishing, a lost or stolen device that touched Cuesoft or client
   data, and any unusual account activity at once to
   [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).
5. Your access to repositories and tools is limited to what your work needs.
   Do not change production systems without the engagement lead’s approval.

## Intellectual property

1. Work product created during an internship is assigned to Cuesoft under
   the internship agreement.
2. For the open-source products, Cuesoft publishes that work under the MIT
   licence: the intern’s authorship remains visible in the public commit
   history.
3. Nothing in the programme assigns work an intern creates outside the
   internship’s scope, on their own time and equipment.

## Open-source licence and contributions

1. The open-source products (Apparule, Expendit and Upstat) are published in
   public repositories under the MIT licence. The licence in each repository
   is the complete statement of anyone’s rights in that code, as the
   [intellectual property terms](../../legal/intellectual-property/) say.
2. When you commit or open a pull request, you promise that you wrote the
   contribution or have the right to submit it, that it does not copy code
   whose licence is incompatible with MIT, and that you have credited any
   third-party code with its licence.
3. Never put secrets, personal data, or confidential Cuesoft or client
   information in a commit, an issue or a pull request.
4. A public commit history is permanent in practice. Other people copy
   repositories, and we cannot recall copies.
5. Do not present a fork or a copy of the code as Cuesoft’s service, and do
   not use Cuesoft’s marks except as the intellectual property terms allow.

## Using AI tools in your work

1. You are responsible for everything you commit, whether you wrote it or an
   AI tool did. AI output can be wrong, insecure or carry licence problems:
   review and test it before it goes into a pull request.
2. Do not put confidential information, secrets, another person’s personal
   data or client data into a third-party AI tool, whose terms you accept

## Conduct and confidentiality

Interns meet the standards of the [handbook](https://handbook.cuesoft.io),
including its anti-harassment, confidentiality and cybersecurity policies.
Programme communication happens on Discord; the
[Privacy Policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) describes what that involves.

## Confidentiality, conduct and Discord in detail

1. **Confidentiality.** The internship agreement carries a non-disclosure
   clause over source code, business plans, client information and everything
   else that is not public, and it survives the internship. The
   [confidentiality terms](../../legal/confidentiality/) and the handbook’s
   [confidentiality policy](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/confidentiality/)
   apply alongside it. Use confidential information only for the work it was
   shared for.
2. **Code of conduct.** The handbook’s
   [anti-harassment policy](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/general/anti-harassment/)
   applies in every channel, repository and meeting. Harassment, dishonesty
   and breach of confidentiality are severe misconduct.
3. **Violations.** The handbook’s
   [violations page](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/people/violation/) sets out
   the steps: a written warning for a first violation, termination for
   repeated violations of the same kind within a year, and immediate
   termination for severe misconduct, with a referral to law enforcement
   where warranted. Violations are recorded in writing and shared with you.
4. **Discord.** The CueLABS™ Discord community is open to anyone. What you
   post there can be seen by its members, and Discord’s own terms apply to
   it. Do not share confidential information in public channels.

## Ending an internship

1. An internship ends at the end of its term unless it is renewed, or earlier
   when either party gives the notice the internship agreement states.
   Cuesoft may end it at once for severe misconduct.
2. On the day it ends, your access to Cuesoft’s email group, Discord,
   repositories, trackers and cloud consoles is revoked. You must return any
   company property and hand over all work product and documentation, and you
   must stop using any access or credentials Cuesoft gave you.
3. The data allowance stops when the internship ends.
4. Confidentiality and the assignment of work product survive the end of the
   internship, and your authorship stays visible in the public commit history.

We may suspend or end your participation or your access to the applicant
portal for breach of these terms or of the internship agreement, unlawful
conduct, or risk to other people or our systems. Where practical we warn
first and give you a chance to put it right. The
[termination page](../../legal/termination/) applies. Intellectual property,
confidentiality, disclaimers, limitation of liability and disputes survive.

go to negotiation and then arbitration in Lagos under the Arbitration and
Mediation Act 2023: see [Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/). If you are
in the United States, you deal with Cuesoft Inc., Delaware law governs and
the Delaware courts decide: see
[United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/). If you are a consumer in
the EU, the EEA or the UK, your local consumer protections are preserved: see
[EU/UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/). The internship agreement’s own law and
forum prevail. Please tell us about a problem at
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) first, so that we can try to
settle it.
