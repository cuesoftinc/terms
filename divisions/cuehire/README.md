## What CueHIRE™ is

CueHIRE™ serves companies through three engagement models: end-to-end
project delivery, managed engineers embedded with your team, and referral
hiring. It is a business-to-business service; it makes no promises to
individual candidates.

## Who may use CueHIRE™

CueHIRE™ is for businesses. If you enquire or sign in for a company, you
confirm that you are at least 18 and have authority to act for it. A person
who is not acting for a business should not use the enquiry form.

## Enquiries

1. The website’s contact form starts a conversation: it does not create

2. Enquiry information is used as the

4. Client materials and data shared during an engagement are handled under

1. Clients and talent who work with CueHIRE™ sign in with their Cuesoft
   account at account.cuesoft.io and come back to a portal. The portal you

   payments of its own company, and talent sees their own contracts, hours

## Invoices and payments

1. Invoices are issued by Cuesoft. An invoice shows its number, the project,
   dates, status, amounts, line items, tax and discount, notes and any
   instalment schedule, and the portal shows the payments and transfers made
   against it. Fees, due dates, currency, taxes, late-payment consequences
   and how a disputed invoice is handled are those of the signed agreement.
   Raise a query with your Cuesoft contact or at
   [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).
2. **Payment plan.** Where Cuesoft offers a plan for an invoice (a deposit and
   instalments, or one payment), the plan you choose is the plan you owe.
3. **Pay by card.** CueHIRE™ has no card form of its own. When you press Pay,
   you are sent to Paystack’s own hosted payment page, where the card is typed.
   The CueHIRE™ website has no field for a card number, expiry date or
   security code, and Cuesoft never receives your card number or security
   code. Paystack processes the payment under its own terms and privacy notice:
   see [Paying an Invoice](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/collection/cuehire/#paying-an-invoice).
   A card payment counts when the platform has asked Paystack and recorded
   that it succeeded.
4. **Saved cards.** You can save a card on your Cuesoft account. Saving it
   starts a card verification of 50 naira, which the platform refunds once the
   card is saved: see the
   [Cards section of the account page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/collection/account/#cards).
   You are responsible for using only a card you are authorised to use.
5. **Pay by bank transfer.** The page shows Cuesoft’s bank details and the
   reference to quote. You then declare the transfer with its reference, an
   optional note and a proof-of-payment file. Cuesoft’s staff review the
   declaration and confirm or reject it with a note, and a transfer counts when
   staff confirm it. Declare only a transfer you have made, and give true
   proof.
6. Receipts are issued for payments recorded against an invoice, and you can
   download them from the portal.
7. If you pay outside the invoice process, the payment may not be applied to
   the invoice.
