# CueTA™: Cuesoft Talent Academy Terms

These terms govern enrolment in and participation in CueTA™, the paid
AI academy operated by Cuesoft. A signed offer of admission or enrolment
agreement prevails over this page wherever they differ.

## Enrolling

1. You enrol in a course on the CueTA™ website and pay through your Cuesoft
   account. There is no application and no consultation call.
2. Cuesoft may refuse an enrolment, or refund it, at its discretion. A
   decision to refuse is made by a person, not by an automated system.
3. You must be at least 18 years old to enrol. Enrolling on behalf of a
   team (Tier 1) requires authority to bind the organisation you name.

## Tuition and payment

1. CueTA™ is a paid academy with **tiered tuition**. The current tiers and amounts
   are stated on the tiers pages of the CueTA™ website.
2. Team enrolments under Tier 1 are priced per staff member.
3. **Enrolling and paying.** You enrol in a course while signed in to your Cuesoft
   account. You pay by card, by the month, with a card saved on your account. Your
   first week of content opens when you are enrolled, and a new week opens every week.
4. **If a payment fails.** Your next weekly release pauses until payment succeeds.
   Content already released stays readable. If payment has not succeeded by the end
   of the grace period stated on the payment screen, your enrolment is revoked.
5. **Cancelling.** You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account.
   You are not charged again, and your courses stay open until the end of the paid period.
6. Where you are admitted under an offer of admission or invoice, that document governs
   payment and prevails over this section.

## Refunds and cancelling

1. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account. Nothing
   more is charged after you cancel.
2. The month you have already paid for is not refunded. Your courses stay
   open until that month ends.
3. Cuesoft may still refund a payment in documented exceptional cases at its
   discretion, or where required by applicable consumer law (see the
   [jurisdiction pages](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/)).
4. If Cuesoft withdraws a course you are enrolled in and cannot offer an
   equivalent alternative, the month you have paid for that course is
   refunded in full.

## The programme

1. Course content, schedule, tooling and instructors may be adjusted to keep
   the curriculum current; material changes to a course you are enrolled in
   are communicated to you.
2. Learner coordination happens through the channels stated at enrolment
   (including WhatsApp groups); the
   [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) describes what that
   involves.
3. **Certificates** are issued on successful completion of a programme, as
   defined by the completion criteria stated for the course. There is no
   separate certificate fee. Certificates state completion: they are not
   professional licences or accreditations.

## Your work and our materials

1. **You own the project work you create as a learner.**
2. Cuesoft owns the curriculum, teaching materials, recordings and
   platform. You may use them for your own learning during and after the
   programme; you may not reproduce, redistribute, resell or teach from
   them without written permission.
3. Contributions you make to Cuesoft’s open-source products follow the
   licence and agreement covering that contribution.
4. Cuesoft may name you as an alumnus and, with your consent, showcase
   your project work; you may withdraw that consent at any time.

## Conduct

Learners meet the standards of the
[handbook](https://handbook.cuesoft.io) values and anti-harassment policy.
Serious misconduct (including plagiarism, harassment and academic
dishonesty) ends participation without refund, after the facts are heard.

## No employment promise

CueTA™ is education. Progression through CueLABS™ or CueHIRE™ is a real
pathway that many learners take, and it is **never a promise of
employment, placement or salary**. No one at Cuesoft is authorised to
promise otherwise, on any channel.
