## Tuition and payment

1. CueTA™ is a paid academy with **tiered tuition**. The current tiers and amounts
   are stated on the tiers pages of the CueTA™ website.
2. Team enrolments under Tier 1 are priced per staff member.
3. **Enrolling and paying.** You enrol in a course while signed in to your Cuesoft
   account. You pay by card, by the month, with a card saved on your account. Your
   first week of content opens when you are enrolled, and a new week opens every week.
4. **If a payment fails.** Your next weekly release pauses until payment succeeds.
   Content already released stays readable. If payment has not succeeded by the end
   of the grace period stated on the payment screen, your enrolment is revoked.
5. **Cancelling.** You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account.
   You are not charged again, and your courses stay open until the end of the paid period.
6. Where you are admitted under an offer of admission or invoice, that document governs
   payment and prevails over this section.

   invoices. We keep invoice and tax records for as long as the law requires:
   see [retention](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/).
6. If you think a charge is wrong, tell us at
   [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) so we can look into it quickly.

7. Paystack processes your payment under its own terms and privacy notice:

## Academic integrity

1. Submit only work that is your own, or that credits its source clearly.
   Plagiarism is presenting another person’s work, words, code or AI output
   as your own.
2. Use AI tools in assessed work only as the course allows, and say so where
   the course asks you to.
3. Do not share, sell, leak or ask for assessment questions, answers, marking
   guides or solutions, except as the course allows.
4. Do not impersonate another person, let another person impersonate you or
   help anyone cheat. Do not falsify attendance, progress, results or a
   certificate.
5. Do not tamper with the weekly release, your progress record or the
   certificate verification page.
6. A breach is serious misconduct under Conduct below, which ends
   participation without refund, after the facts are heard.
