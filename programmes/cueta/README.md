# CueTA™ — Cuesoft Talent Academy Terms

These terms govern applications to and participation in CueTA™, the paid
AI academy operated by Cuesoft. A signed offer of admission or enrolment
agreement prevails over this page wherever they differ.

## Applying

1. Admission starts with an application on the CueTA™ website. Submitting
   an application creates no contract: it is a request to be considered.
2. Every application begins with a **free consultation call**. No payment
   is requested before it, and the call carries no obligation on either
   side.
3. Admission decisions are made by the CueTA™ leadership on the
   application and the consultation, and are at Cuesoft's discretion.
   Application decisions are made by humans, not automated systems.
4. You must be at least 18 years old to apply. Applying on behalf of a
   team (Tier 1) requires authority to bind the organisation you name.

## Tuition and payment

1. CueTA™ is a paid academy with **tiered tuition**. The current tiers and
   amounts are stated only on the application page of the CueTA™ website;
   no other page or channel states pricing.
2. Team enrolments under Tier 1 are priced per staff member.
3. Tuition is invoiced and paid off-site under your offer of admission or
   invoice — **never through the website**. The website takes no payment
   and stores no payment details.
4. An offer of admission lapses if the tuition it states is not paid by
   the date it states; a lapsed offer creates no obligation.

## Refunds and deferrals

1. Tuition is **non-refundable once a cohort begins**, except in
   documented exceptional cases at Cuesoft's discretion or where required
   by applicable consumer law (see the
   [jurisdiction pages](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/)).
2. Before a cohort begins, refund requests are honoured in full, less any
   transaction costs actually incurred.
3. Cuesoft may allow a paid learner to defer to a later cohort once,
   subject to seat availability; a deferral is not a refund right.
4. If Cuesoft cancels a cohort and cannot offer an equivalent alternative,
   tuition already paid for that cohort is refunded in full.

## The programme

1. Cohort content, schedule, tooling and instructors may be adjusted to
   keep the curriculum current; material changes to a running cohort are
   communicated to that cohort.
2. Cohort coordination happens through the channels stated at enrolment
   (including WhatsApp groups); the
   [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) describes what that
   involves.
3. **Certificates** are issued on successful completion of a programme, as
   defined by the completion criteria shared with the cohort. There is no
   separate certificate fee. Certificates state completion — they are not
   professional licences or accreditations.

## Your work and our materials

1. **You own the project work you create as a learner.**
2. Cuesoft owns the curriculum, teaching materials, recordings and
   platform. You may use them for your own learning during and after the
   programme; you may not reproduce, redistribute, resell or teach from
   them without written permission.
3. Contributions you make to Cuesoft's open-source products follow the
   licence and agreement covering that contribution.
4. Cuesoft may name you as an alumnus and, with your consent, showcase
   your project work; you may withdraw that consent at any time.

## Conduct

Learners meet the standards of the
[handbook](https://handbook.cuesoft.io) values and anti-harassment policy.
Serious misconduct — including plagiarism, harassment and academic
dishonesty — ends participation without refund, after the facts are heard.

## No employment promise

CueTA™ is education. Progression through CueLABS™ or CueHIRE™ is a real
pathway that many learners take, and it is **never a promise of
employment, placement or salary**. No one at Cuesoft is authorised to
promise otherwise, on any channel.
