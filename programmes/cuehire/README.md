# CueHIRE™ — Talent and Project Management Agency Terms

These terms govern use of the CueHIRE™ website and the start of a client
relationship. Every actual engagement runs under a signed proposal or
agreement, which prevails over this page wherever they differ.

## What CueHIRE™ is

CueHIRE™ serves companies through three engagement models: end-to-end
project delivery, managed engineers embedded with your team, and referral
hiring. It is a business-to-business service; it makes no promises to
individual candidates.

## Enquiries

1. The website's contact form starts a conversation — it does not create
   an engagement, a reservation of capacity, or any obligation.
2. Enquiry information is used as the
   [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) describes.
3. Responses, indicative scopes and verbal estimates are not offers; only
   a written proposal signed by both parties binds either side.

## Engagements are written

1. Every engagement runs under a written proposal or agreement stating
   scope, capacity, service levels, fees, invoicing and terms.
2. Proposals are itemised; fees are priced against the brief and are
   **not published on the website**.
3. Service levels (response and effort-start times, sprint capacity,
   escalation paths) are those stated in the signed agreement — nothing on
   the website constitutes a service-level commitment.
4. Client materials and data shared during an engagement are handled under
   the engagement's confidentiality and data terms.

## No guarantees on the website

Nothing on the CueHIRE™ website promises hiring outcomes, delivery dates,
placements, rates or salaries — to companies or to candidates. Talent
recommended through CueHIRE™ (including CueTA™ alumni and CueLABS™
interns) is put forward on merit, and every engagement decision is the
client's own.
