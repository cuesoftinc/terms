  infringing or deceptive content.
- **Forms start conversations.** Submitting an application or contact form
  creates no contract and no obligation on either side; a binding
  relationship starts only with a signed agreement or written offer.
- **Third-party links** (GitHub, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, Medium,
  client properties and others) lead to services we do not control, under
  their own terms.
- **Availability.** We aim to keep the Websites available but do not
  guarantee uninterrupted access, and we may change, suspend or retire any
  part of them at any time.
