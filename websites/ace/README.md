Ace answers questions about Cuesoft using approved company information. Ace
can make mistakes: its answers are provided as-is, are not advice, and do
not form contractual commitments: the authoritative answer to anything
material is the relevant page, agreement or a reply from
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

- Do not submit sensitive personal information through the chat; the
  [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) explains how chat input is
  processed.
- Do not use the assistant to generate or transmit unlawful, infringing or
  deceptive content.
- We may rate-limit or withdraw the assistant at any time.

## What Ace is, and what its answers are

Ace is an AI assistant. Everything it generates is **AI output**: an answer,
a draft, a summary or any other content an AI feature produces for you.

1. **AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.** Ace can state
   something false with confidence, can miss what matters and may not know
   recent changes. Check it against the relevant page, agreement or a reply
   from [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) before you act on it.
2. **Do not rely on it for important decisions.** Do not use AI output as
   the basis for a legal, financial, medical, employment, immigration or
   safety decision, or for any decision with serious consequences for you or
   anyone else. Ask a qualified professional.
3. **A person should review it.** Read and, where it matters, correct AI
   output before you use, publish, send or act on it.
4. **You are responsible for how you use it.** You decide whether and how to
   use AI output, and you answer for the result, including what you do with
   it in your own work or for your own customers.
5. **It may not be unique.** Ace may give the same or a similar answer to
   other people. We do not promise that AI output is original, or that it is
   free of third-party rights.
6. **It does not bind us.** Ace is not authorised to make offers, accept
   orders, promise prices, dates or outcomes, waive a term or give a
   commitment on behalf of Cuesoft or anyone else. Only a signed agreement
   or a written reply from us does that.

## What you must not do

In addition to the points above, you must not:

- type in sensitive personal information, passwords, card details, identity
  numbers, health information or anything you have no right to share;
- try to make Ace ignore its rules ("jailbreaking"), or to extract its system
  instructions, configuration or the information it holds for other users;
- use Ace to generate or spread unlawful, infringing, deceptive, harassing
  or harmful content, malware, or content that sexualises minors;
- use Ace, or its output, to train or tune another model, or to build a
  competing assistant, without our written permission;
- run Ace automatically, at scale or at a rate that burdens it, or use
  several accounts or sessions to get round a limit;
- present AI output as the advice of a professional, or as a statement from
  Cuesoft.

Our [acceptable use policy](../#acceptable-use-policy) applies to Ace in full.
